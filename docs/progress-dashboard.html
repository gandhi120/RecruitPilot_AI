What is this project?

RecruitPilot AI is a voice assistant for a real phone number. When a recruiter calls Varun and he's unavailable, the AI answers the call, honestly introduces itself as an AI, asks permission, then interviews the recruiter — which company, what role, what tech stack, what salary range, remote or not. It can check Varun's calendar, email his resume, and save the recruiter's details, all mid-call.

After the call ends, it writes a full transcript and a structured summary, saves everything to a database, and notifies Varun immediately. If the same recruiter calls again, it remembers them. Varun watches it all on a live web dashboard.

The hard part isn't the conversation — it's speed. On a phone call, a silence longer than ~1.5 seconds feels like the line went dead, so every stage of the system must stream its output before the previous stage has finished.

“Hello. You've reached Varun Gandhi's AI Assistant. Varun is currently unavailable. With your permission, I can collect information regarding this opportunity and immediately notify him.”
